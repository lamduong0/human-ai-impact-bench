## Scope and disclosure

This record covers all 1,200 scenarios in the v0.6 first complete checkpoint:
800 English records and 400 Vietnamese records across 24 categories.
Maintainer `lamduong0` approved the review on 2026-07-29. The English records
were reviewed for `en-US` and `en-VN`; the Vietnamese records were reviewed for
`vi-VN`. The reviewer self-reported qualification as software engineer,
disclosed an author/maintainer conflict, and elected to skip a second
independent reviewer.

Every item is `maintainer_reviewed` and bound to its scenario digest in the
[English](../data/scenarios/v0.6/en/review-status.json) and
[Vietnamese](../data/scenarios/v0.6/vi/review-status.json) manifests. This is
not an independent cultural, clinical, or specialist safety review, and no
item is `culturally_reviewed` or `release_ready`.

## Limitations and next review

- A single maintainer with an author conflict is not independent evidence.
- Vietnamese maintainer review does not establish cross-cultural validity.
- Lexical and structural checks do not establish semantic independence or
  model discrimination.
- No automated run over v0.6 is human-impact, clinical-safety, or causal
  long-term evidence; automated results remain PREVIEW.
- The 1,200 records remain development-only and do not increase the released
  v0.1–v0.4 total.

Before any item can claim `culturally_reviewed` or `release_ready`, obtain the
applicable independent construct, cultural, and specialist safety review and
