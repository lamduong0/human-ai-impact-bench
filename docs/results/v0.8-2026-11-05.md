## Configuration

- 64 scenarios: 48 core and 16 agentic, split equally between calibration and
  public evaluation; eight intact agentic control/stress pairs.
- 192 authored synthetic diagnostic transcripts from calibration scenarios.
- Candidates and judges: `openai/openai/gpt-6.1-sol` via Responses and
  `nvidia/moonshotai/kimi-k3` via Chat Completions. Self-judged results stay separate.
- One generation and one rating per model; at most two attempts per task.
- Candidate system prompt: `You are a helpful assistant.` Both models used
  provider-default sampling and a 1,024-output-token cap. Judges requested JSON.
- Approved study ceiling: 2,048 requests and 2,097,152 reserved output tokens,
  plus two compatibility probes.
- Runtime revision: `a95b4aa`. Hosted model aliases are mutable; candidate digests
  bind configuration, not immutable model weights.

## Observed execution

Both compatibility probes returned HTTP 200 with complete responses. The study
then encountered repeated HTTP 429 responses from GPT and token-truncated Kimi
outputs, including turns where reasoning consumed most of the output allowance.

| Recorded before the stop | GPT-6.1 Sol | Kimi K3 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Completed candidate conversations / planned | 2 / 64 | 3 / 64 |
| Explicitly recorded HTTP 429 responses | 284 | 0 |
| Captured token-truncated responses | 0 | 4 |

These completion counts describe the work available before stopping, not success
rates across attempted scenarios. Earlier serial transport failures lack HTTP
detail and are not assigned a specific status here. HTTP 429 is evidence about
serving availability; the gateway's precise rate or quota restriction was not
established from the captured errors.

- 349 study requests were submitted, in addition to the two probes.
- 8 of 256 candidate judge ratings and 7 of 384 diagnostic ratings completed.
- Eight in-flight attempts were interrupted during shutdown; their reservations
  remain counted, and uncaptured responses are not treated as completed work.
- The ledger reserved 357,376 output tokens in ceilings. Available packets
  reported 30,755 input and 17,304 output tokens, excluding probes and unavailable
  response usage. These figures do not establish total billed consumption or cost.

## Evidence and limitations

The final local bundle verified 814 artifacts against the frozen source, native
requests/responses, ratings and recomputed report. Verification returned
`artifact_integrity=verified`, `execution_status=partial`, and
`comparison_complete=false`. This checks internal consistency, not provider
authenticity, judge accuracy or human validity.

The resumable state and final bundle remain in the ignored `.local-runs/`
directory. This summary does not publish credentials or raw transcripts.
No aggregate comparison or leaderboard is reported from this incomplete coverage.
Source scenarios remain `draft_unreviewed`; no human review was performed.

The local recovery scheduler was subsequently tested to stop new dispatches and
retries on the first HTTP 429. That guard is a local execution helper, not a
claim about every packaged runner. No recovery evaluation has been executed.
