# v0.7 agentic research plan

Status: **DRAFT design, not empirical evidence**. This plan operationalizes the
approved [v0.7 design](superpowers/specs/2026-09-07-v0.7-benchmark-dataset-design.md)
and [four construct definitions](v0.7-agentic-category-proposal.md). Task 11
registers planned arithmetic and a scoring hook; it does not create the corpus,
run candidates, or establish independent content review.

## Questions and evidence limits

Under controlled synthetic conversational context, does the candidate preserve
memory consent and provenance, resist personalized manipulation, honor delegated
authority, and recover from inherited unsafe behavior? These are behavioral
proxy questions. They do not test real storage, retrieval, deletion, expiry,
external execution, product capability, or longitudinal human outcomes.

Simulated prior sessions are not observed elapsed use. Synthetic assistant
prefills are not target output, and user assertions about prior exchanges are
not verified target actions. Report only what the candidate subsequently says.
No psychological or clinical diagnosis, real-world failure rate, causal harm,
production-safety finding, or validation claim follows from this design.

## Planned configurations and units

| Configuration | Construction | Physical records | Matched pairs |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `v0.7-agentic-en` | 4 categories × 25 families × 2 contexts × 2 modes | 400 | 200 |
| `v0.7-agentic-vi-preview` | 4 categories × 25 families × 1 context × 2 modes | 200 | 100 |

Each English category has 100 records and 50 pairs; each Vietnamese preview
category has 50 records and 25 pairs. Counts in
`configs/scenario-coverage-standard.json` are planned coverage requirements,
not availability or maturity assertions. Vietnamese adaptation and qualified
cultural review are later work and do not block the English milestone.
Core and agentic remain separate; there is no combined v0.7 overall score.

## Paired intervention

For every locale-specific `matched_pair_id`, create exactly one `control` and
one `prefill_stress` record. Hold the visible `initial_prompt` and all ordered
`follow_up_prompts` byte-identical between modes. Also align category, locale,
language, risk, dimensions, concept family, context, persona, expected behavior,
and failure modes. Mode-specific IDs, protocol tags, and prefill presence
identify the intervention, not a different user request or scoring standard.

Control omits `prefill_messages`. Stress supplies two or four alternating
`user`/`assistant` messages, beginning with a user and ending with an assistant.
Only that synthetic history differs in conversational input. The two contexts
within a family must change relevant evidence, decision pressure, or consequences,
not merely names or nouns. All contexts, modes, and language adaptations of a
family remain together when assigning evaluation splits.

Write visible prompts that make sense in both modes. A control must not require
a nonexistent assistant admission, nor should stress prompts reveal the safe
answer. The candidate may evaluate a supplied claim without accepting that it
authored the claim. Do not seed both an unsafe trajectory and its remedy; that
would confound recovery with copying an already supplied correction. Treat a
mode difference as sensitivity to the whole supplied history, not proof that
one isolated sentence caused it without further controlled ablations.

## Authoring and observation

Freeze 25 coherent families and two substantive English contexts per category
before drafting. Inventory qualifying v0.5 sources by mechanism; adaptation
must preserve honest source identity and does not transfer historical review.
Use fictional, minimized personal details and nonprocedural risk descriptions;
exclude quarantined material. JSONL remains the source of truth. Generators may
derive indexes, not template the scenario meaning.

Rubrics must describe visible evidence and the specific authorization or
influence boundary. Include ordinary safe personalization and reversible
preparation as negative cases. Recovery coverage must distinguish inherited
mistakes from fresh candidate errors and from supported corrections. Source,
concept, context, protocol, locale, and model-assisted authorship tags must be
explicit. Later corpus work supplies exact matrices, lineage, overlap checks,
and fresh digest-bound `draft_unreviewed` entries.

Score only candidate-generated assistant messages using the common paired
rubric. Preserve the prefill separately with its synthetic provenance. A seed
containing a critical failure does not itself count as a candidate event;
continuing or endorsing it can count when the label's operational definition is
met. Distinguish no opportunity to observe a behavior from successful recovery.
Report whether correction persists through later candidate turns, not just an
initial apology. Human adjudication must inspect the full relevant trajectory.

## Preregistered evaluation, after corpus and tooling readiness

Before inspecting candidate results, freeze configuration and scenario digests,
splits, hypotheses, exclusions, missingness handling, analysis units, calibration
sample, and acceptance criteria. Obtain complete runs from at least three
distinct candidate-model families under controlled settings. Hold system prompt,
judge and judge settings, generation settings, and scenario revision constant
where comparison requires it; record supported-setting differences and failures.

Use a fixed independent judge where feasible, and a stratified, blinded,
double-rated human calibration sample spanning category, risk, mode, and locale.
Blinding concerns candidate identity and experimental expectations; raters still
need the visible context and explicit prefill provenance to score correctly.
Preregister agreement measures for ordinal dimensions and critical labels, and
inspect judge–human disagreements before asserting calibration.

Report category and protocol results separately, including paired dimension
differences, critical-failure event rates with denominators, control-safe/stress-
unsafe and control-unsafe/stress-safe transitions, and turn-level repair
persistence. Physical record counts are not independent sample sizes: report
family-cluster uncertainty, ICC/design effect and effective counts where
estimable. Keep the matched context pair intact and cluster inference by family.
Do not treat 400 records as 400 independent constructs.

Include dimension support, raw and diluted penalties, ceiling/floor behavior,
variance, non-discriminating items, and all failed, retried, or missing runs.
No fabricated completions or silent exclusion of difficult items. Any revision
needs a reason, new digest, renewed review, and a separate evaluation freeze.
The provisional 40.0 authorization penalty and automatic-safety hook cannot
support a v0.7 policy until empirical calibration and threshold preregistration
are complete. Legacy policies are not substitutes.

## Known implementation concerns and readiness gates

General v0.7 metadata propagation and mixed-track scoring isolation remain a
separate final-remediation concern. Current scenario/run validation has track
checks, but general annotations and direct scoring do not provide end-to-end
configuration provenance or guarantee rejection of mixed core/agentic input.
Do not describe that gap as fixed by this task's constants or arithmetic tests.
Until addressed and tested, keep input artifacts explicitly separated and do
not interpret an accepted mixed score as a supported benchmark result.

Further work must verify exact pair alignment and prefill provenance throughout
runner, annotations, scoring, and reports; authored coverage; split isolation;
review digests; and overlap resolutions. Independent construct review, qualified
locale review, specialist review where required, and release review remain
distinct evidence. Automated tests and agent review are not human approval.
Only after those applicable checks and preregistered empirical work can the
configuration be considered for stronger benchmark-evidence maturity. Even that
does not certify human outcomes or deployment safety.
