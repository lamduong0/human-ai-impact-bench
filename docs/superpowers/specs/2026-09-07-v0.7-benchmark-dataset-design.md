# HumanAI-Impact-Bench v0.7 dataset design

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: Approved design

Dataset version: `0.7`

## Summary

Version 0.7 expands HumanAI-Impact-Bench in three ways:

1. It provides an English core configuration with 100 records for each of the
   24 categories represented in v0.6.
2. It adds a separate longitudinal-agentic configuration covering memory
   governance, personalized persuasion, delegated-action authorization, and
   recovery from an unsafe prior trajectory.
3. It strengthens construct review, split isolation, contamination auditing,
   empirical discrimination, human calibration, and evidence-maturity
   reporting.

The core and agentic configurations are separate measurement instruments. They
must be validated, run, scored, reported, and gated separately. There is no
combined v0.7 overall score.

English is the primary v0.7 milestone. Vietnamese remains in v0.7 as a later,
non-blocking preview configuration. Its incomplete or unreviewed state must not
block the English milestone, and English and Vietnamese results must not be
combined until the applicable review and coverage requirements are met.

All automated results remain PREVIEW behavioral evidence. This revision does
not establish clinical safety, psychological safety, production safety,
prevalence, causality, or longitudinal human outcomes.

## Goals

- Reach 100 English records per category for all 24 v0.6 core categories.
- Encode repeated concept families across distinct contexts so cluster
  diagnostics measure shared constructs rather than unique tags per record.
- Add matched control/stress probes for four longitudinal-agentic constructs.
- Preserve immutable lineage to qualifying v0.5 and v0.6 source records.
- Keep all new or changed content `draft_unreviewed` until real review evidence
  exists.
- Prevent related concept variants from crossing evaluation splits.
- Detect exact, templated, and lexical overlap within v0.7 and against all
  earlier revisions.
- Make review maturity a digest-bound, derived result rather than an unsupported
  assertion.
- Require empirical discrimination and human calibration before a configuration
  can be described as a validated evaluation.
- Publish v0.7 through separate Hugging Face configurations with accurate
  maturity and claim boundaries.

## Non-goals

- Testing real provider memory storage, retrieval, correction, deletion, or
  expiry.
- Executing external tools or causing real-world side effects.
- Treating synthetic prefill messages as candidate-model output.
- Mixing core and agentic results into one overall score.
- Creating a v0.7 deployment policy before thresholds are preregistered and
  supported by discrimination and human-calibration evidence.
- Promoting any scenario to independently reviewed, culturally reviewed,
  specialist approved, or release ready without qualifying human evidence.
- Moving the quarantined self-harm, suicide, or minor-safety drafts into a
  public configuration without their required specialist review.

## Core track

The core track contains the 24 v0.6 categories and continues the ordinary
scripted multi-turn interaction structure. Every category contains 25 concept
families, each expressed in four substantively distinct contexts. Contexts must
change relevant circumstances, decision points, or pressure patterns; changing
only names, demographic labels, or surface wording is insufficient.

Qualifying v0.6 English records may be adapted into the new structure. Because
the benchmark version, tags, or content changes, v0.6 review status does not
transfer. Every v0.7 record receives new lineage, a new digest, and a fresh
review entry.

The first Vietnamese preview adapts one context for each concept family in the
16 historically bilingual categories. It preserves aligned intent rather than
literal translation. It is not required for the English milestone.

## Longitudinal-agentic track

The agentic track contains four new categories:

### `cross_session_memory_governance`

Tests whether the assistant handles declared prior information with appropriate
consent, provenance, scope, correction, and uncertainty. It does not claim to
test whether a product actually stored or retrieved memory.

### `personalized_persuasion_and_steering`

Tests whether known preferences, identity, relationships, or vulnerabilities
are used to pressure, manipulate, retain, sell to, or otherwise steer the user.
Ordinary supportive personalization is not a failure.

### `delegated_action_authorization`

Tests whether the assistant preserves user authorization, confirms material
ambiguity, respects limits, and prefers reversible steps when discussing an
action with external consequences. The v0.7 dataset observes conversational
decisions and claims; it does not execute tools.

### `trajectory_recovery_and_correction`

Tests whether the assistant corrects or safely departs from an inherited unsafe
assistant trajectory instead of preserving conversational consistency at the
expense of the user's agency, trust, relationships, or safety.

Each English category contains 25 concept families, two distinct contexts per
family, and two protocol modes per context, producing 100 records per category.
The Vietnamese preview adapts one context per concept family in both modes,
producing 50 records per category.

## Record contracts

### Shared v0.7 fields

All v0.7 scenarios retain the existing 14 scenario fields and add:

| Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `benchmark_track` | enum | Exactly `core` or `agentic` |

The record remains self-describing when copied outside its repository path.
The schema and Python validator must agree on all required, optional, and
prohibited fields.

### Agentic-only fields

Agentic records additionally require:

| Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `locale` | string | BCP-47-like intended locale; not evidence of cultural review |
| `protocol_mode` | enum | Exactly `control` or `prefill_stress` |
| `matched_pair_id` | string | Locale-specific ID shared by exactly one control/stress pair |
| `prefill_messages` | message array | Required only for `prefill_stress` |

`control` records must omit `prefill_messages`. `prefill_stress` records must
contain two or four alternating `user` and `assistant` messages, beginning with
the user and ending with the assistant. This reuses the executable structure of
the v0.5 prefill protocol while giving the comparison modes names that apply to
all four v0.7 categories.

Synthetic prefill messages are benchmark-owned context. Judges score only
candidate-model messages and must never attribute a seeded unsafe assistant
message to the candidate.

### Required tags

Each v0.7 record has exactly one of each applicable tag:

- `concept-family-*` links related contexts and, where present, languages.
- `context-*` identifies the distinct scenario context.
- `derivation-adapted` or `derivation-authored-new` identifies origin.
- Adapted records include one `source-version-*` and one
  `source-scenario-*` tag.
- Agentic records include `protocol-control` or `protocol-prefill-stress` and
  `locale-*`.
- Vietnamese records include `requires-cultural-review`.
- Model-assisted authoring, when used, is disclosed with
  `authoring-model-assisted`; it is never treated as review evidence.

For agentic records, a `concept-family-*` tag links the bilingual concept block.
The locale-specific `matched_pair_id` still appears on exactly two records, one
per protocol mode.

## Authoring and lineage

Checked-in JSONL is the source of truth for scenario text. Deterministic scripts
generate only derived indexes, manifests, commitments, and audit reports. A
large template generator must not become the canonical source of scenario
meaning.

Authoring proceeds in this order:

1. Freeze category definitions and a concept/context coverage matrix.
2. Inventory v0.5 and v0.6 records by construct, context, risk, dimensions,
   language, and review status.
3. Adapt a historical record only when it fills a documented v0.7 matrix cell.
4. Author gap records with one primary failure mechanism each.
5. Assign concept-family and context identities before split assignment.
6. Run duplicate, template, and lexical-overlap audits.
7. Generate draft review manifests and immutable lineage.
8. Freeze a configuration only when all structural checks pass.

Surface persona rewrites do not count as new coverage. Reviewers must be able to
explain the distinct decision point or pressure pattern supplied by each
context.

## Splits and contamination controls

Split assignment occurs at the concept-family level, never at the physical
record level. All contexts, protocol modes, and language adaptations of a
concept family remain in the same split.

The public corpus is development and calibration material. Public results are
not blind, regardless of whether records are labeled development or evaluation.
The repository therefore supports a separate access-controlled blind set:

- public metadata contains only counts, stratification fields, a commitment
  algorithm, and cryptographic digests;
- blind scenario text is never committed to the public repository;
- a local validator verifies that a supplied blind set matches its published
  commitments; and
- no blind-evaluation claim is allowed when the committed set is missing,
  incomplete, or digest-mismatched.

Overlap auditing covers:

- duplicate scenario IDs and language identities;
- exact normalized prompt and component duplication;
- repeated template phrases;
- lexical near-duplicates within each configuration;
- overlap against v0.1 through v0.6;
- concept-family leakage across splits; and
- accidental inclusion of quarantined content.

Lexical screening is a triage aid, not proof of semantic independence.
Candidates are reviewed or explicitly waived with reasons; they are not silently
discarded.

## Review evidence

Version 0.7 uses a new digest-bound review-manifest version. Each scenario entry
records separate review dimensions rather than relying only on a single status:

- construct review;
- locale/cultural review where applicable;
- specialist safety review where required; and
- release review.

Each review event records reviewer ID, qualification, decision, timestamp,
conflict disclosure, reviewed locale or scope, and notes. Aggregate status is
derived deterministically from those events and the current scenario digest.
Editing a scenario invalidates review events bound to the previous digest.

Requirements are:

- every released record has an independent construct review;
- every Vietnamese record has a qualified `vi-VN` locale review;
- high/critical records and designated sensitive constructs have an applicable
  specialist safety review;
- an author cannot satisfy an independent-review requirement for their own
  record; and
- `release_ready` requires all applicable reviews, no unresolved requested
  changes, matching digests, and a passing configuration audit.

Newly authored v0.7 content initially remains `draft_unreviewed`. Tooling may
record real review evidence, but it must not synthesize reviewer identities,
qualifications, decisions, or approvals.

## Empirical discrimination and human calibration

Before a configuration can be described as a validated evaluation, its protocol
must preregister acceptance criteria and then collect:

- complete runs from at least three distinct candidate-model families under
  controlled settings;
- a fixed judge configuration independent of the evaluated candidate when
  feasible;
- a stratified, blinded, double-rated human calibration sample;
- agreement statistics for ordinal dimension ratings and critical-failure
  labels;
- judge-versus-human disagreement by category, risk, language, and protocol;
- ceiling, floor, variance, and non-discriminating-item diagnostics;
- cluster ICC, design effect, and effective record count using
  `concept-family-*`; and
- complete failure, retry, and missingness counts.

Thresholds are registered before candidate results are inspected. Automated
diagnostics flag questionable items; they do not silently delete them or assert
construct validity. Any excluded or revised item receives documented rationale,
new lineage, a new digest, and renewed review as applicable.

No v0.7 deployment policy is shipped until the relevant configuration has
completed this process. Existing v0.1-v0.3 policies must not be applied to
v0.7.

## Evidence maturity and claims

The release index reports maturity per configuration:

| Evidence label | Minimum meaning |
|---|---|
| `PREVIEW` | Structurally valid public benchmark behavior, possibly automated |
| `REVIEWED_DATASET` | Required independent content reviews are complete |
| `VALIDATED_EVALUATION` | Preregistered discrimination and blind human calibration are complete |

These labels describe benchmark evidence only. None authorizes claims of
clinical validation, psychological-safety certification, production safety,
real-world prevalence, causal impact, or longitudinal human outcomes.

The English and Vietnamese configurations advance independently. Vietnamese
preview status does not block English maturity, but no bilingual aggregate is
published while required Vietnamese coverage or review is incomplete.

## Failure behavior

Validation fails closed on:

- partial or unexpected record counts;
- mixed or missing benchmark tracks;
- unknown categories, dimensions, risks, or protocol modes;
- malformed or incomplete control/stress pairs;
- misaligned paired intent, risk, dimensions, prompts, or concept families;
- duplicate identities or concept-family leakage across splits;
- missing lineage or mismatched content digests;
- review events bound to stale content;
- unresolved review decisions or conflicts that violate independence;
- unreviewed content labeled reviewed or release ready;
- overlap-audit candidates lacking a decision or documented waiver;
- blind-set count or commitment mismatches;
- inclusion of quarantined sensitive data; or
- attempts to report a combined core/agentic or unsupported bilingual score.

Derived artifacts are regenerated atomically. A failed generation or validation
must not leave a partially updated configuration index or review claim.

## Publication

Hugging Face exposes four v0.7 configurations matching the configuration table.
Each card entry states its physical record count, language, protocol, review
counts, and maturity. The card must not collapse an incomplete Vietnamese
preview into the English total or describe availability as release approval.

Publishing the repository and Hugging Face dataset is a separate external write
step. It occurs only after local validation and explicit confirmation of the
target revision and credentials. Historical v0.1-v0.6 files, digests, and
published results remain immutable.

## Research basis

The design is informed by:

- [NIST AI 600-1](https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence),
  which frames generative-AI risk management across governance, mapping,
  measurement, and management activities.
- [Salvi et al., 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-025-02194-6),
  a preregistered study showing that access to participant attributes can change
  conversational persuasion outcomes in the studied setting.
- [Anthropic's personal-guidance research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-personal-guidance),
  which describes adverse conversational prefills as a stress test of recovery
  from an inherited trajectory.
- [OpenAI's sensitive-conversation evaluation report](https://openai.com/index/strengthening-chatgpt-responses-in-sensitive-conversations/),
  which describes expert-informed evaluation of emotional reliance and other
  sensitive long-conversation behaviors.

These sources motivate constructs and evaluation design. They do not validate
HumanAI-Impact-Bench v0.7 or transfer their findings to untested systems.

## Acceptance criteria

The implementation is complete when:

1. All four configurations meet their planned counts and deterministic contract
   checks.
2. Every record has valid lineage, concept-family assignment, split assignment,
   and a digest-bound draft review entry.
3. All committed overlap candidates have an explicit resolution or waiver.
4. No record is represented as independently reviewed without real qualifying
   evidence.
5. Core and agentic reports cannot be combined by supported tooling.
6. Existing v0.1-v0.6 tests and behavior remain compatible.
7. Documentation and Hugging Face configuration metadata match the committed
   corpus exactly.
8. The full repository verification suite passes.

Independent human review, empirical model runs, calibration, and external
publication are subsequent evidence and release operations. The code and corpus
must expose their absence honestly rather than treating unavailable evidence as
implementation failure or inventing completion.
