# v0.6 Stage 1 review record

Date: 2026-07-28 · Status: **MAINTAINER REVIEWED / INDEPENDENT REVIEW PENDING**

## Scope

This record covers the 210 English scenarios in the v0.6 Stage 1 development
foundation: 30 records for each of the seven v0.4 categories. Maintainer
`lamduong0` approved the review on 2026-07-28 for `en-US` and `en-VN`,
self-reported a software-engineer qualification, disclosed an
author/maintainer conflict, and elected to skip a second independent reviewer.
All 210 records are `maintainer_reviewed`; this is not an independent cultural,
clinical, or specialist safety review. The selection and scenario digests are
frozen in
[`stage1-selection.json`](../data/scenarios/v0.6/stage1-selection.json).
Records added for the 1,200-record checkpoint are outside this approval and
remain `draft_unreviewed`.

## Checks performed

- schema validation against the legacy 14-field contract;
- exact count, category, scenario-ID, concept-tag, and context-tag checks;
- source-digest verification for all 56 adapted records;
- exact prompt/component duplicate screening;
- lexical near-duplicate candidate generation within v0.6 and for the 154
  authored-new records against v0.1-v0.5;
- cross-review by an agent that did not author the category under review;
- prompt cue-leakage, observable-transition, risk, dimension, sensitive-content,
  and category-boundary review; and
- digest-bound review-manifest generation after the scenario files were frozen.

The reproducible lexical report is
[`stage1-lexical-similarity-audit.json`](../data/scenarios/v0.6/stage1-lexical-similarity-audit.json).
Its default thresholds are 0.58 for full prompt-sequence similarity and 0.45
for token Jaccard similarity.

## Material findings resolved

- Rewrote systemic rubric cue leakage in newly authored engagement and moral
  outsourcing prompts.
- Removed follow-ups that falsely assumed the candidate had already produced
  an unsafe response.
- Replaced or differentiated repeated constructs across compulsive use,
  engagement manipulation, ideological steering, moral outsourcing, romantic
  boundaries, and v0.5 stateful tracks.
- Changed the romantic consent-withdrawal probe so the user clearly stops twice
  and never re-consents.
- Instantiated previously hypothetical state transitions, including a neutral
  work request after a persistent-flirtation request and a clear withdrawal
  after requested verbal hostility.
- Corrected risk and dimension assignments where the observable stakes or
  mechanism did not support the original labels.
- Limited product-state scenarios to claims and persuasion observable in a
  synchronous chatbot transcript.

## Known limitations

- The 56 adapted records preserve their v0.4 substantive content. Inherited
  high-cue prompts remain useful for lineage comparison but should not be
  treated as strong discrimination evidence.
- Lexical screening cannot establish semantic independence. Human construct
  review may still identify overlaps that automated thresholds miss.
- English authoring does not establish `en-US`, `en-VN`, Vietnamese-language,
  or broader cultural validity.
- No model-discrimination run has yet established whether each scenario
  separates stronger and weaker deployed systems.
- Automated transcript scores remain PREVIEW engineering evidence and do not
  measure causal or long-term human impact.
