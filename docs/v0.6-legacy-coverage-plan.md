# v0.6 legacy-category coverage plan

Drafted: 2026-07-28 · Status: **FIRST 1,200-RECORD CHECKPOINT MAINTAINER REVIEWED**

> **Reproducibility rule:** released v0.1–v0.4 files, review manifests,
> checksums, Hugging Face revisions, and gate policies remain immutable. This
> plan creates a new version; it never rewrites an old benchmark result.

## Objective

Version 0.6 will provide a mature 100-record view for every category that has
scenario data in v0.1–v0.4. The goal is balanced coverage and easier category
comparison, not a claim that 100 synthetic records estimate production
prevalence or human outcomes.

The legacy releases contain 24 populated categories:

- eight v0.1 human-impact categories;
- eight v0.2 reality-testing categories;
- `trust_and_anthropomorphism`, introduced in the v0.3 expansion; and
- seven v0.4 AI-initiated or outcome-side categories.

The 22 quarantined sensitive v0.4 drafts are not part of this plan. They remain
excluded until their dedicated safety review is complete.

## Standard record unit

The frozen Stage 1 development foundation contains **30 records per category**
for the seven v0.4 categories. The current complete checkpoint contains **50
physical scenario records per category** across all 24 categories; the mature
config targets **100**:

- At the first checkpoint, bilingual categories use 25 culturally adapted
  concept pairs and English-first categories use 50 English records.
- At maturity, bilingual categories use 50 culturally adapted concept pairs
  and English-first categories use 100 English records.
- A category may have a larger archival source corpus, but the standard config
  selects exactly 100 records with a frozen, documented selection rule.

Across 24 populated legacy categories, the first checkpoint contains **1,200
records** and the mature standard contains **2,400 records**. These are
balanced evaluation views, not replacements for the full historical datasets.

The immutable source inventory is generated by
[`scripts/generate_v06_source_inventory.py`](../scripts/generate_v06_source_inventory.py)
and stored in
[`data/scenarios/v0.6/source-inventory.json`](../data/scenarios/v0.6/source-inventory.json).

## Why this is a new version

Changing v0.1–v0.4 in place would invalidate:

- dataset and transcript digests;
- existing PREVIEW comparison reports;
- review-manifest coverage;
- published GitHub and Hugging Face revisions; and
- deployment-policy provenance.

Version 0.6 receives a new dataset digest, review manifest, benchmark version,
and gate policy. Reports must identify whether they used a historical version,
the v0.6 standard config, or a larger archival config.

## Construction rules

1. Inventory every legacy record by category, language, concept, context,
   risk, dimensions, and review status.
2. Run semantic and deterministic duplicate checks before authoring anything
   new. Reuse an exact source record only when it fills a distinct cell.
3. Fill coverage gaps with one primary construct per scenario. Do not multiply
   superficial persona rewrites to reach 100.
4. For bilingual categories, author culturally adapted EN/VI concepts rather
   than literal translations. Preserve aligned intent, risk, expected behavior,
   failure mode, and scored dimensions.
5. Add lineage tags such as `source-version-v0-2`,
   `source-scenario-<id>`, `concept-<id>`, and `context-<id>`. A copied record
   keeps its source text; an adapted record gets a new scenario ID.
6. Keep all newly authored or adapted records `draft_unreviewed`. Prior review
   does not automatically transfer to a modified scenario or a new locale.
7. Keep v0.6 on the legacy 14-field interaction contract. Stateful v0.5
   control/recovery fields are not silently added to ordinary legacy probes.

## Staged authoring

### Stage 1: v0.4 categories

The seven cleanly category-structured v0.4 tracks first established a
30-record English development foundation in v0.6: **210 records**, including
56 source-preserving adaptations and 154 newly authored records. That exact
digest-bound selection was reviewed first; the 140 later additions were
subsequently included in the disclosed full-checkpoint maintainer review. Each
track now contains 50 `maintainer_reviewed` records. Later reviewed waves may
advance them to 80 and then 100 records.

The machine-assisted author/reviewer pass, resolved findings, and remaining
human-review requirements are recorded in the
[Stage 1 review record](v0.6-stage1-review.md).

### Stage 2: v0.1 and v0.2 bilingual categories

The first checkpoint contains 25 structurally aligned EN/VI concept pairs for
each of the 16 populated bilingual categories, contributing 800 physical
records. All 800 are `maintainer_reviewed`; this single-maintainer status is
not an independent cultural-review result, and qualified locale review remains
required. Later waves may advance to 40 and then 50 aligned pairs per category.

### Stage 3: v0.3-only trust coverage and consolidation

The first checkpoint contains 50 English `trust_and_anthropomorphism` records:
2 source-preserving adaptations and 48 newly authored records. The frozen
1,200-record checkpoint index consolidates all three stages. All 50 trust
records are `maintainer_reviewed`. Later reviewed waves may advance this
category and the full index to 80 and 100 records per category. Existing v0.3
categories are never truncated in their historical version; only the v0.6
views use documented selections.

## Required validation

- the frozen Stage 1 selection contains exactly 7 categories and 30 records per
  category;
- the current checkpoint contains exactly 24 categories and 50 physical
  records per category;
- exactly 25 English and 25 Vietnamese records for every bilingual category;
- unique scenario ID and language identities;
- valid legacy 14-field scenario contract;
- explicit lineage and concept/context tags;
- no unresolved exact or near-duplicate concepts inside a category;
- complete `draft_unreviewed` review-manifest coverage before review starts;
- separate dataset and gate digests from v0.1–v0.5; and
- full pytest, Ruff, format, schema, credential, and diff checks.

Automated results remain PREVIEW evidence. The balanced record count does not
establish cultural validity, clinical safety, production safety, or causal
human impact.

Authoring follows 50-, 80-, and 100-record checkpoints from the
[scenario coverage standard](scenario-coverage-standard.md). Each checkpoint
requires a fresh construct, duplicate, review, and model-discrimination audit.
